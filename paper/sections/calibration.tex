\section{Calibration}
\label{sec:calibration}

\paragraph{The plan's calibration claim (A2) fails at the \lA\% level.}
Table~\ref{tab:a2} applies the plan's rule to each of V2's cells on
held-out accounts:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{\R{a2/count/fail} cells fail}, their bootstrap interval
  wholly outside the band: the \lA\% interval at every horizon, and the
  \lB\% interval at \hB\ days;
\item \textbf{\R{a2/count/pass} cells pass};
\item \textbf{\R{a2/count/inconclusive} cell is inconclusive}: the \lD\%
  interval at \hA\ days, whose interval straddles the band's edge.
\end{itemize}
V2's PIT histogram is hump-shaped at \hB\ and \hC\ days
(Figure~\ref{fig:pit}), consistent with its over-covering central
intervals: the cone is too wide in its middle. The original model V0 fails
the other way: its \lC\% interval covers \R{ho/var/V0/90/cov90}\% at \hC\
days, and its histogram is U-shaped.

\paragraph{What the PIT histograms can show.} Each forecast is stored as
quantiles at fixed levels, and its PIT inverts the piecewise-linear
quantile function. An outcome beyond the outermost stored quantile gets a
PIT of exactly 0 or 1 (for V2 at \hA\ days, \R{xh/s10/pit/V2/7/share_pit1}\%
of outcomes get 1). An outcome on a flat stretch, an atom of the forecast,
gets the midpoint of the levels it spans rather than a randomized value.
The histograms therefore show the direction of a dispersion error, which
the coverage at every level (Table~\ref{tab:a2}) confirms. They cannot
certify calibration: a uniform PIT is necessary, not sufficient, for it
\citep{gneiting2007calibration}. The selection rule tested the \lC\% level only, which
is why a variant that misses the \lA\% level was selected.

\paragraph{V1 is the best-calibrated variant.} The conformal recalibration of
V0 brings every level near nominal (Figure~\ref{fig:coverage} in the Appendix). Its \lA\%
interval covers \R{ho/var/V1/7/cov50}, \R{ho/var/V1/30/cov50} and
\R{ho/var/V1/90/cov50}\%, and its \lC\% interval \R{ho/var/V1/7/cov90},
\R{ho/var/V1/30/cov90} and \R{ho/var/V1/90/cov90}\%. It lost the selection
on CRPS: its account-mean skill against B-SA+ is \R{ho/var/V1/30/skill}
and \R{ho/var/V1/90/skill} at \hB\ and \hC\ days, against V2's
\R{ho/var/V2/30/skill} and \R{ho/var/V2/90/skill}. Its adjusted levels
crossed in \R{xd/s2/crossings/all/share_non_monotone}\% of development
forecasts and were sorted into a monotone function.
