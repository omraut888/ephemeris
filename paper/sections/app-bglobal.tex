\section{B-GLOBAL without calendar look-ahead (exploratory)}
\label{app:bglobal}
The original B-GLOBAL was trained on development origins of all dates, so
for early held-out origins it saw outcomes from their future. We retrained
it two ways, on development data only, with the frozen features,
hyperparameters and levels:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{G-cf (calendar-forward):} for each quarter, a model trained
  on development rows whose outcome precedes the quarter's start, predicting
  the held-out origins in that quarter;
\item \textbf{G-cut:} one model per horizon, trained on development rows
  whose outcome precedes a single cutoff, predicting held-out origins on or
  after it (\R{xh/s7-cut/V2-vs-B-SA+/30/forecasts} origins at \hB\ days).
\end{itemize}
On held-out accounts, account-mean skill at \hB\ and \hC\ days:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Against the original B-GLOBAL:} G-cf
  \R{xh/s7-cf/B-GLOBAL-cf-vs-B-GLOBAL/30/skill_mean} and
  \R{xh/s7-cf/B-GLOBAL-cf-vs-B-GLOBAL/90/skill_mean} (development:
  \R{xd/s7-cf/B-GLOBAL-cf-vs-B-GLOBAL/30/skill_mean} and
  \R{xd/s7-cf/B-GLOBAL-cf-vs-B-GLOBAL/90/skill_mean}); G-cut
  \R{xh/s7-cut/B-GLOBAL-cut-vs-B-GLOBAL/30/skill_mean} and
  \R{xh/s7-cut/B-GLOBAL-cut-vs-B-GLOBAL/90/skill_mean}.
\item \textbf{Against B-EQ:} G-cf
  \R{xh/s7-cf/B-GLOBAL-cf-vs-B-EQ/30/skill_mean} and
  \R{xh/s7-cf/B-GLOBAL-cf-vs-B-EQ/90/skill_mean}; G-cut
  \R{xh/s7-cut/B-GLOBAL-cut-vs-B-EQ/30/skill_mean} and
  \R{xh/s7-cut/B-GLOBAL-cut-vs-B-EQ/90/skill_mean}.
\item \textbf{V2 against G-cf:}
  \R{xh/s7-cf/V2-vs-B-GLOBAL-cf/30/skill_mean} and
  \R{xh/s7-cf/V2-vs-B-GLOBAL-cf/90/skill_mean}.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Its calibration is weaker.} G-cf's \lC\% interval covers
\R{xh/s7/stats-cf/B-GLOBAL-cf/30/cov90} and
\R{xh/s7/stats-cf/B-GLOBAL-cf/90/cov90}\% at \hB\ and \hC\ days, against
V2's \R{xh/s7/stats-cf/V2/30/cov90} and \R{xh/s7/stats-cf/V2/90/cov90}\% on
the same origins (\R{xh/s7-cf/V2-vs-B-GLOBAL-cf/30/forecasts} per horizon).
Intervals for every comparison are in
\texttt{paper/results/exploratory-heldout/s7-bglobal/}.

A model trained across other people's accounts and shipped frozen to a
device would keep most of this advantage. Whether that is acceptable is a
product question this paper does not settle.
