\section{Reproducing the paper}
\sloppy
Two levels of reproduction are possible:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Rebuild from committed artifacts.} Every result file the text
  quotes is committed under \texttt{paper/results/}.
  \texttt{scripts/paper.sh --pdf-only} regenerates the numbers, the tables
  and this PDF from them, without the raw data. Every number in the text
  is read from those files by \texttt{paper/analysis/macros.py}.
\item \textbf{Recompute from the raw data.}
  \texttt{scripts/fetch\_paper\_data.py} downloads Berka and MoneyData, and
  \texttt{scripts/paper.sh} recomputes every development, synthetic,
  MoneyData and identity result, then the figures and the PDF. The script
  first checks the files against pinned SHA-256 checksums
  (\texttt{paper/data.sha256}); for the two transaction files they are:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{berka/trans.tsv}:\\
    \texttt{\scriptsize\R{sha/berka/trans.tsv}}
  \item \texttt{moneydata/transactions.tsv}:\\
    \texttt{\scriptsize\R{sha/moneydata/transactions.tsv}}
  \end{itemize}
  The held-out evaluation is not rerun by that script. It ran once,
  behind its guard, and recomputing it requires the guarded command with
  a logged reason.
\end{itemize}
The protocol, every decision and its date, and the claims as first written
are in \texttt{paper/PLAN.md}.
