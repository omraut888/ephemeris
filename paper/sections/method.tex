\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{State, inputs and observations}
The state is $x_t = [b_t, c_t, m_t]^\top$: the balance, the discretionary
spending rate per day, and the expected paycheck. On a daily grid,
\begin{equation}
x_{t+1} = A x_t + B u_t + w_t,\quad
A = \begin{bmatrix}1&-1&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{bmatrix},\;
B = \begin{bmatrix}1\\0\\0\end{bmatrix},\;
w_t \sim \mathcal N\!\left(0, \operatorname{diag}(\sigma_s^2, q_c, q_m)\right).
\end{equation}
Paychecks and scheduled bills are \emph{known} flows: they enter the
balance through $u_t$ at their actual amounts. A paycheck's amount is also
an observation of $m$ with $H_{\text{inc}} = [0\;0\;1]$ and variance
$R_{\text{inc}}$. The bank's balance is observed through
$H_{\text{bal}} = [1\;0\;0]$. An adapter tags each record as income,
scheduled, discretionary or an own-account transfer from the data alone.
The primary income stream is the largest regular inflow, and a bill is a
recurring outflow at a regular interval. Matched transfer pairs cancel.

Bank feeds contain pending records that later post at a different amount
or date. The tracker therefore keeps a \emph{settled checkpoint}, through
the day before the earliest pending record, and replays the provisional
tail on every sync. The tail never adapts the noise estimates. None of the
real data sets here has a pending state, so this machinery is exercised
only on synthetic data.

\subsection{Adaptive noise, the gate and lumps}
Each noise channel (the balance channel $\sigma_s^2$ and the income channel
$R_{\text{inc}}$) has one unknown variance. Every innovation $\nu$ gives a
one-sample estimate, for example $\hat v = \nu^2 - h P^- h^\top$ for the
income channel. It is unbiased only if the innovation model is correct and
before the gate, the weighting and the clamp below are applied; after them
it is a heuristic estimate. These are folded into an exponentially forgetting weighted
mean,
\begin{equation}
n \leftarrow \lambda n + w,\qquad v \leftarrow \operatorname{clamp}\!\left(v + w\,(\hat v - v)/n\right),
\end{equation}
in the spirit of covariance matching \citep{mehra1970,myers1976}. The
weight $w = (v/S)^2$ discounts samples taken while the state is still
uncertain (innovation variance $S \gg v$). The discounted count $n$ is an
effective number of samples, not a derived degrees-of-freedom parameter.

Spending is heavy-tailed: a rent deposit or a car repair is not Gaussian
noise. When a balance observation's normalized innovation exceeds a gate,
the excess is treated as an unexplained \emph{lump} flow, added to $b$
before the update, and the clipped remainder updates the filter normally.
The lumps are recorded, and the forecast later resamples them.

\subsection{Regime changes}
When the model holds, the normalized innovations $e = \nu/\sqrt S$ are white
with unit variance. A step in spending or pay biases them. A two-sided CUSUM
\citep{page1954},
\begin{equation}
g^+ \leftarrow \max(0, g^+ + e - \kappa),\qquad
g^- \leftarrow \max(0, g^- - e - \kappa),\qquad \text{alarm when } g > h,
\end{equation}
runs on the gate-clipped innovations of each channel. On an alarm, the
run's mean shift $\hat\Delta$ becomes a zero-mean jump of variance
$\hat\Delta^2$ in $c$ (capped at a multiple of $\hat\sigma_s^2$) or in $m$.
The matching noise estimate forgets back to its prior count, so the filter
relearns the new level quickly.

\subsection{The Monte Carlo cone}
Each path starts from $x_0 \sim \mathcal N(\hat x, P)$ and draws its own
$\sigma_s^2$ and $R_{\text{inc}}$ from scaled inverse-$\chi^2$ distributions
whose degrees of freedom are set to the effective counts $n$. That choice
is a heuristic, not a posterior: it makes a thin history spread the cone.
Each simulated day applies
\begin{equation}
b \mathrel{+}= -c + \varepsilon + \ell + \text{income} + \text{bills},\qquad
c \mathrel{+}= \eta_c,\qquad m \mathrel{+}= \eta_m,
\end{equation}
with $\varepsilon \sim \mathcal N(0,\sigma_s^2)$. A lump $\ell$ occurs at
the smoothed empirical rate of absorbed lumps, with its size resampled from
them and mixed with a prior; this puts back the tail the gate trimmed.
Paychecks land on the detected schedule plus a sampled offset, moved to
the previous business day of the feed's holiday calendar, with amount
$m + \mathcal N(0, R_{\text{inc}})$. Bills recur on their day of the month
or at their period plus a sampled drift.

\paragraph{Spend floor.} The filter's $c$ is Gaussian and can sit below
zero, which would have a path earn money every day. With a floor (by default, zero), $c_0$ is drawn from its Gaussian truncated below at the floor by
inverse CDF, and the random walk reflects at the floor. The draw factors
$P$ in the order $(c, b, m)$, so the correlation between $b$ and $c$ is
kept.

\paragraph{Determinism.} Each path uses its own counter-based ChaCha8
stream, so a path's trajectory does not depend on the number of paths or
the loop order. Transcendental functions are meant to come from the
pure-Rust \texttt{libm} \citep{libm_crate}. \S\ref{sec:identity} reports
where that intention failed. We use \R{c/paths} paths and report quantiles
on a midpoint grid, from which CRPS is computed.

\subsection{The revision candidates}
\label{sec:variants}
The original model is V0. Its failure on the development split
(\S\ref{sec:protocol}) triggered a revision. The candidates and the rule
for choosing among them were committed before any revision code was
written.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{V1: online conformal recalibration} of V0 by adaptive
  conformal inference \citep{gibbs2021aci}, per account and horizon. Each
  of \R{c/aci-levels} central levels keeps its own recursion with step
  $\gamma = \R{c/aci-gamma}$, capped at \R{c/aci-max}. The calibration
  stream is V0's own forecasts from a grid of origins every
  \R{c/aci-grid-step} days from day \R{c/aci-grid-start}, using only
  outcomes known at the forecast origin.
\item \textbf{V2: sparse spending.} For an account whose median number of
  discretionary records per complete month, over the \R{c/sparse-window}
  days before the origin, is below \R{c/sparse-max}, discretionary flows
  are a compound Poisson process. Each path draws a rate
  $\lambda \sim \mathrm{Gamma}(n + \tfrac12, \text{rate} = D)$, the
  Jeffreys posterior for $n$ events in $D$ days. Each day then has
  $N \sim \mathrm{Poisson}(\lambda)$ events whose signed amounts are
  resampled from the window. This replaces $-c + \varepsilon$ and the lump
  draws. The engine has the option off by default; it is selected per
  account by the rule above.
\item \textbf{V3: empirical-Bayes priors} for $c$, $m$ and the two noise
  variances, fitted to the other accounts. On development these come from
  the other folds of \R{c/folds}; on held-out, from all development
  accounts.
\item \textbf{V4:} V2 and V3 combined, recalibrated as in V1.
\end{itemize}
