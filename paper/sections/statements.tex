\section*{AI assistance}
\label{sec:statements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{AI assistance}
The implementation, the experiments and the first draft of this paper were
produced with Anthropic's Claude Code, working under the author's
direction. The author made the protocol decisions: the analysis plan and
its thresholds, the approvals of adapter deviations, the revision and
selection rule, the freeze, and the decisions to run the held-out
evaluation and each post-hoc analysis. The author reviewed the results and
the text. This is why some commits in Table~\ref{tab:timeline} follow each
other within minutes.

\section*{Code and data availability}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Code and data availability}
The code, the analysis plan, the committed results and the scripts that
build this paper are at \url{https://github.com/omraut888/ephemeris}, which
will be made public before this paper is posted. The raw Berka and
MoneyData files are not redistributed. \texttt{scripts/fetch\_paper\_data.py}
fetches them, and their pinned checksums are in \texttt{paper/data.sha256}.
