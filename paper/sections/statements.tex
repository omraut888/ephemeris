\section*{Code and data availability}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Code and data availability}
The code, the analysis plan, the committed results and the scripts that
build this paper are in a public repository,
\url{https://github.com/omraut888/ephemeris}. This version is the tagged
release \url{https://github.com/omraut888/ephemeris/releases/tag/v1.0.1-paper}.
The raw Berka and MoneyData files are not redistributed;
\texttt{scripts/fetch\_paper\_data.py} fetches them, and their pinned
checksums are in \texttt{paper/data.sha256}. Appendix~\ref{app:repro}
gives the commands that rebuild the paper and recompute the held-out
results.
